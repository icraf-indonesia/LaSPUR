# LaSPUR Domain Glossary

Land and Seascape Planning Unit Reconciliation (LaSPUR) is a spatial decision support platform for harmonising terrestrial spatial plans (RTRW) and marine/coastal zoning plans (RZWP3K).

## Language

### Spatial Units & Plans

**Planning Unit (PU)**:
The fundamental spatial polygon unit used as the common geographic denominator across land and sea for analysis and allocation.
_Avoid_: Grid cell, pixel, plot

**RTRW**:
Rencana Tata Ruang Wilayah; the official Indonesian terrestrial spatial plan governing land allocations and zoning.
_Avoid_: Land plan, terrestrial zoning

**RZWP3K**:
Rencana Zonasi Wilayah Pesisir dan Pulau-Pulau Kecil; the official Indonesian marine and coastal zoning plan.
_Avoid_: Marine plan, sea zoning

### Conflict Analyses

**Overlap Analysis**:
Spatial intersection analysis that detects physical overlaps and slivers between land-use designations and coastal zones.
_Avoid_: Intersection analysis, overlay conflict

**Adjacency Analysis**:
Spatial topology analysis detecting potential boundary incompatibilities between neighboring or abutting planning zones.
_Avoid_: Neighbor analysis, border conflict

**Interdependent Analysis**:
Spatial analysis evaluating functional, ecological, or systemic interdependencies across land and seascape planning units.
_Avoid_: Interconnection conflict, dependency analysis

**Compatibility Matrix (Matriks Keserasian)**:
A cross-tabulated evaluation matrix defining whether pairs of adjacent or overlapping RTRW and RZWP3K designations are compatible, conditional, or conflicting.
_Avoid_: Lookup table, mapping crosswalk

### Suitability Indicators (PADU)

**PADU Index**:
The composite spatial suitability and harmony index synthesized from individual sub-indices through multi-criteria weighting.
_Avoid_: Composite score, suitability rank

**PADU-KE (Kesesuaian Ekologi)**:
Ecological suitability index assessing land use/land cover (LULC) adjacency and ecological condition.
_Avoid_: Eco score

**PADU-HS (Habitat Satwa)**:
Wildlife habitat suitability index based on species presence, corridors, and environmental tolerances.
_Avoid_: Wildlife score, biodiversity index

**PADU-SE (Sosial Ekonomi)**:
Socio-economic suitability index based on demographic pressure, accessibility, and community infrastructure.
_Avoid_: Social score

**PADU-KL (Kawasan Lindung)**:
Protected area compliance index evaluating alignment with conservation and protected zone regulations.
_Avoid_: Conservation score

**PADU-KH (Komposisi Habitat)**:
Habitat composition and presence index evaluating habitat coverage (e.g. mangrove, coral reef) across land and seascape units.
_Avoid_: Habitat presence, coastal habitat score

**PADU-RTp (Rawan Bencana)**:
Disaster risk and environmental vulnerability index evaluating hazards like fires, floods, and landslides.
_Avoid_: Risk score, hazard index

**PADU-KI (Kesesuaian Infrastruktur)**:
Infrastructure compatibility index evaluating proximity and access to existing and planned infrastructure.
_Avoid_: Infra score

### Decision & Resolution

**Reconciliation (Rekonsiliasi)**:
The systematic optimization and negotiation process that resolves spatial conflicts by ranking alternative zoning allocations against economic NPV and PADU indicators.
_Avoid_: Harmonization, merge process, resolving conflicts
